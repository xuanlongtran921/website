1. The Swiss-Chinese Heritage Behind Project 304

In 1961, the Ministry of Light Industry of China initiated a top-secret task known as Project 304: building the nation’s first aviation chronograph wrist watch for the People's Liberation Army Air Force pilots. Rather than reverse-engineering, Tianjin Watch Factory (which would later evolve into Sea-Gull) formally purchased the complete blueprints, machinery, and manufacturing tooling for the celebrated Venus 175 column-wheel chronograph movement from Switzerland.

Today, that exact horological lineage survives as the Sea-Gull Calibre ST1901. While Swiss brands now charge $4,000 to $12,000 for column-wheel mechanical chronographs, Sea-Gull manufactures and regulates this authentic historic movement entirely in-house for a fraction of that cost.
